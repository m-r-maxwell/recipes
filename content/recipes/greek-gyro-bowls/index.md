### 512 Cals | 42g Protein | 20g Fat | 34g Carbs
### 4 Servings

# Ingredients:
- 600g lean ground turkey
- 200g jasmine rice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- salt
- black pepper
- 150g cucumber
- 150g cherry tomatoes
- 40g feta cheese

## Tzatziki
- 200g plain Greek yogurt
- 75g grated cucumber
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp dill

# Steps
1. Cook the rice.
2. Mix together all of the tzatziki ingredients.
3. Season the turkey with oregano, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, cinnamon, salt, and pepper.
4. Cook the turkey until browned.
5. Chop the cucumber and tomatoes.
6. Crumble the feta.

# Serve
Add 125g cooked rice, turkey, cucumber, tomatoes, and feta to each container. Spoon the tzatziki over the top or keep it separate.
