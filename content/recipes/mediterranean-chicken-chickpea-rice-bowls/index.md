### Macros Per Serving (4 Total)

### 480 Calories | 43g Protein | 51g Carbs | 10g Fat

# Ingredients

- 1¼ lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
- ¾ cup dry basmati rice
- 1 cup canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- ½ red onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley

# Steps

1. Cook the basmati rice according to the package directions.
2. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
3. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
4. Toss the chicken with half of the olive oil, smoked paprika, oregano, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
5. Toss the zucchini, bell pepper, red onion, and chickpeas with the remaining olive oil.
6. Spread the chicken and vegetables across a large sheet pan.
7. Roast for 18–22 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the chicken reaches 165°F.
8. Mix the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, and parsley.
9. Add a splash of water to the yogurt sauce if needed.
10. Divide the rice, chicken, vegetables, and chickpeas among four meal prep containers.
11. Store the yogurt sauce separately and add it after reheating.
