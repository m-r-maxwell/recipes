### 496 Cals | 24g Protein | 16g Fat | 55g Carbs
### 4 Servings

# Ingredients:
- 250g dried brown lentils
- 600g potatoes
- 15g butter
- 60ml milk
- 1 onion
- 2 carrots
- 2 celery stalks
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 250ml vegetable broth
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp rosemary
- 1 tbsp gluten free Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- salt
- black pepper

# Steps
1. Boil the potatoes until fork tender.
2. Mash with the butter and milk.
3. Cook the lentils according to the package.
4. Dice the onion, carrots, celery, and garlic.
5. Cook the vegetables in olive oil until softened.
6. Stir in the tomato paste.
7. Add the broth, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, rosemary, and lentils.
8. Simmer until thick.
9. Spread into a baking dish.
10. Top with the mashed potatoes.
11. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes.
12. Broil for 2-3 minutes until lightly browned.

# Serve
Cut into 4 equal portions and transfer to meal prep containers.
