### 523 Cals | 43g Protein | 18g Fat | 39g Carbs
### 4 Servings

# Ingredients:
- 600g chicken breast
- 700g sweet potatoes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- salt
- black pepper
- 15g butter
- 30g hot honey
- parsley

# Steps
1. Dice the sweet potatoes.
2. Toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Roast at 425°F for 25-30 minutes.
4. Season the chicken with the remaining spices.
5. Cook over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes per side.
6. Add the butter.
7. Stir in the hot honey and coat the chicken.
8. Simmer for 2 minutes until sticky.

# Serve
Divide the sweet potatoes evenly between 4 containers and top with approximately 150g of chicken.
