### 548 Cals | 45g Protein | 21g Fat | 40g Carbs
### 4 Servings

# Ingredients:
- 600g chicken breast
- 225g gluten free penne
- 120g light cream cheese
- 30g parmesan
- 200ml chicken broth
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp lemon pepper seasoning
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- parsley

# Steps
1. Cook the pasta according to the package.
2. Season the chicken with lemon pepper and Italian seasoning.
3. Cook the chicken in olive oil until golden.
4. Remove the chicken from the pan.
5. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
6. Stir in the chicken broth and cream cheese.
7. Mix until smooth.
8. Add the parmesan and lemon juice.
9. Return the chicken to the pan.
10. Stir in the cooked pasta.

# Serve
Divide evenly into 4 containers and garnish with parsley.
